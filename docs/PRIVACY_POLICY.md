# Shieldra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Shieldra is an anti-theft app that the phone's owner installs on their own phone.
This policy matches what the app does; the same text is in the app under Settings → Privacy policy.

## What Shieldra records
Only when a guard you turned on fires, or when you ask from your own Telegram chat: photos from the
cameras, a short audio clip, the phone's location, and device status such as battery, network and SIM
changes. Events and evidence are stored on the phone. Evidence files are encrypted with a key held in
the Android Keystore that never leaves the phone.

## Where it can go
Only to the Telegram bot and the email account that you set up yourself in the app. Shieldra has no
server of its own: it does not upload your data to the developer or to any other party.

## What Shieldra never does
No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of you, no selling or sharing of data. Location is read only
while protection is armed or when you ask, and goes only to your own channels.

## Why it asks for permissions
- Camera and microphone: evidence after an incident.
- Location: where the phone is.
- Device administrator: to notice failed unlock attempts.
- Notifications: to keep protection running and show alerts.
- Screen lock (biometric / device credential): to confirm it is you for export, deletion and app lock.

## Shieldra Pro
Payment is handled by Google Play. Shieldra only learns whether you own Pro; it never sees your card or
account details.

## Your control
Export evidence as an encrypted file; events are deleted automatically after 90 days and evidence after
30 days; lock the app with your screen lock; delete all data at any time in Settings → Delete all data.
Uninstalling Shieldra removes everything it stored.

## Children
Shieldra is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact
Use the developer contact shown on the app's Google Play page.
