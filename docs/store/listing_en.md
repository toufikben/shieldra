# Google Play listing — English

**App name:** Shieldra — Anti-theft guard

**Short description (80):** Silent anti-theft protection. Alerts go only to your own Telegram and email.

**Full description:**
Shieldra quietly guards your phone and tells you — and only you — when something is wrong.

When a guard fires, Shieldra captures evidence and sends it to the Telegram bot and email account you set
up yourself. There is no Shieldra server, no account, no analytics and no ads.

GUARDS
• Failed unlock attempts
• SIM change
• Battery / power-off tamper
• Motion (table mode): grace period, siren, then capture
• Safe zones: alert when the phone leaves every place you marked as safe
• Countdown guard from 1 minute to 7 days

EVIDENCE
• Front and back camera photos, a short audio clip and the location
• Stored on your phone, encrypted with a key that never leaves it
• Real photo viewer, audio player and map link inside each event
• Export as a passphrase-protected encrypted file, confirmed with your screen lock

REMOTE COMMANDS (from your own Telegram chat)
/locate, /photo, /alarm, /stopalarm, /track on|off, /lost, /found, /status

PRIVACY
• App lock with your screen lock; hidden from the recents screen when on
• Automatic cleanup: events after 90 days, evidence after 30 days
• Delete all data at any time

SHIELDRA PRO (subscription)
Every evidence type and channel: camera photos, audio, instant Telegram alerts, remote commands,
live location trail and table mode. The free plan sends your location by email.

Shieldra must be allowed to run in the background, use the camera, microphone and location, and be a device
administrator to notice failed unlocks. Each permission is explained in the app before it is requested.
